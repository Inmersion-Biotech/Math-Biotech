# Community code of conduct

We want a respectful, technically rigorous and inclusive space for scientific learning and software engineering.

Participants are expected to communicate clearly and considerately, welcome good-faith questions, give evidence-based feedback, respect privacy and intellectual property, and credit contributions. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, unsolicited disclosure of personal information and deliberate misinformation are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, limit participation or close interactions that violate these expectations. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers; do not publicly expose personal information.

This policy applies to project discussions, issues, reviews and other spaces represented as part of the project.
